---
title: "Liveness Checks"
description: "Enhanced verification for UBOs or Individuals."
---

Users and businesses from high risk regions can be onboarded with additional compliance measures, and must complete a hosted identity and liveness check before the user can be onboarded.

<Tip>
  Check out [High Risk Regions](/v3/compliance/regions#high-risk-regions) for the countries that apply and the supported user types.
</Tip>

## Requirements

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card title="10% UBO threshold" icon="users">
    All UBOs with 10% ownership or above must complete full individual KYC before the entity can be approved.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Hosted liveness check" icon="camera">
    Qualifying UBOs must complete biometric liveness verification.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
