<Update label="August 10, 2026" description="v3 Now in Beta">
  **v3 Beta**

  v3 is a full rebuild of the HIFI API, now available in beta:

  - **[Users](/v3/core/users/overview) & [Compliance](/v3/core/compliance/overview)** – create individual/business users, submit KYC/KYB, track review status with actionable RFIs, and use hosted [Compliance Links](/v3/core/compliance/compliance-link) for onboarding
  - **[Wallets](/v3/core/wallets/overview)** – provision HIFI custodial wallets, check balances and deposits, whitelist [external wallets](/v3/core/wallets/external-wallet), and manage [Deposit Offers](/v3/core/wallets/deposit-offers) on Canton
  - **[Counterparties](/v3/core/counter-party/overview)** – manage payment recipients/payers with linked external accounts and cards
  - **[Transactions](/v3/core/transactions/overview)** – [onramps](/v3/core/transactions/onramps), [offramps](/v3/core/transactions/offramps), [onchain transfers](/v3/core/transactions/onchain-transfer) (including batch), [swaps](/v3/core/transactions/swap), and [bridging](/v3/core/transactions/bridge)
  - **[Orchestration](/v3/core/orchestration/overview)** – [Virtual Accounts](/v3/core/orchestration/virtual-accounts) that auto-mint stablecoin on deposit, and [Orchestration Addresses](/v3/core/orchestration/orchestration-address) that auto-offramp onchain deposits
  - **[Eligibility](/v3/core/eligibility/overview)** – check supported [corridors](/v3/core/eligibility/corridors) and [Virtual Account routes](/v3/core/eligibility/virtual-account-routes) before creating transactions
  - **[Webhooks](/v3/core/webhooks/overview)** – manage endpoints, resend events, and subscribe to the full event catalog
  - **[Transaction Approvals](/v3/features/transaction-approval)**, **[Developer Fees](/v3/features/developer-fee)**, and **[Settlement Rules](/v3/features/settlement-rule)** – admin approval workflows and fee configuration

  See the [v3 docs](/v3/docs/introduction/welcome) to get started.
</Update>
